Frequently Asked Questions

If you need assistance, feel free to reach out to us via email at support@playenet.com. Alternatively, you can contact us directly through the support button on the in-game settings page.
To save your game progress, simply use the "Sign In" option available in the game settings page. This will securely store your progress.
If you wish to request data deletion, please get in touch with us through the support button in the game settings page. We'll assist you with the process.
Our game is available on both Android and iOS devices. You can download it from the Google Play Store or the Apple App Store.
Yes, our game is completely free to play! While there are optional in-app purchases available, you can enjoy the game without spending any money.
You can play the game offline for most features. However, certain actions like saving progress, accessing updates, or making in-app purchases may require an internet connection.
If you’ve made a purchase but it’s not showing up, please use the Sign in option available in the game settings. Make sure you're signed in to the same account used for the purchase.
The game language is the same as your device's language. If you change the language of your device, the game will automatically switch to that language.
If you experience any issues, please reach out to our support team by using the "Support" button in the settings page or emailing us at support@playenet.com. Provide as much detail as possible to help us resolve the issue quickly.
Yes, you can play on multiple devices! Just make sure you're signed into the same account on each device to sync your progress.
